# NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 — Source Guide

> Joseph Technologies independent source guide. Rev. 3 superseded SP 800-61 Rev. 2 in April 2025.

## Why this publication matters

SP 800-61 Rev. 3 treats incident response as part of cybersecurity risk management and expresses recommendations through a CSF 2.0 Community Profile. This shifts governance away from treating incident response as an isolated technical plan.

## Use it to

- assign incident-response governance and decision authority;
- incorporate preparation into Govern, Identify, and Protect activities;
- connect detection, response, and recovery activities;
- define coordination, communications, escalation, and improvement expectations;
- evaluate incident-response outcomes within the wider risk program.

## Governance questions it should answer

- Who declares and leads an incident?
- Who determines materiality, legal notification, and executive escalation?
- Which internal and external parties must coordinate?
- What evidence and decisions must be recorded?
- How are lessons learned converted into risk and control improvements?

## Important source locations

- Section 2: incident-response recommendations mapped to CSF 2.0 functions.
- Govern outcomes: strategy, roles, policy, oversight, and supply-chain considerations.
- Detect, Respond, and Recover outcomes: analysis, communications, mitigation, and restoration.
- Appendix A: incident-response CSF 2.0 Community Profile.
