# NIST SP 800-37 Rev. 2 — Source Guide

> Joseph Technologies independent source guide. Use the linked NIST publication as the controlling reference.

## Why this publication matters

SP 800-37 Rev. 2 defines the Risk Management Framework (RMF): a structured life-cycle process that connects system-level security and privacy decisions to organization-level risk management. It establishes accountability for categorization, control selection and implementation, assessment, authorization, and continuous monitoring.

## RMF lifecycle

1. Prepare the organization and system to manage risk.
2. Categorize the system and information.
3. Select and tailor controls.
4. Implement controls and document how they operate.
5. Assess control implementation and effectiveness.
6. Authorize operation based on risk information.
7. Monitor changes, controls, and risk continuously.

## Use it to design

- governance roles and risk decision authority;
- system authorization processes;
- control ownership and inheritance;
- assessment and remediation workflows;
- POA&M governance;
- continuous-monitoring and ongoing-authorization processes.

## Do not treat it as

- a universal legal mandate outside its applicable federal context;
- a control catalog—the controls are primarily in SP 800-53;
- a guarantee that completing documents produces acceptable risk.

## Important source locations

- Chapter 2: risk management concepts and organization-wide perspective.
- Chapter 3: RMF steps and tasks.
- Appendix A: roles and responsibilities.
- RMF Prepare tasks: organization- and system-level preparation.
- Authorize tasks: risk analysis, determination, and authorization decision.
- Monitor tasks: ongoing assessment, reporting, and authorization maintenance.
