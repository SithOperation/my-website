# NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — Source Guide

> Joseph Technologies independent source guide. This guide does not reproduce the control catalog.

## Why this publication matters

SP 800-53 Rev. 5 provides a flexible catalog of security and privacy controls. Organizations select and tailor controls based on mission, business needs, threats, privacy risk, laws, policies, and other requirements. It is designed to operate within an organization-wide risk-management process.

## Use it to

- define security and privacy control requirements;
- assign control ownership and implementation responsibility;
- develop system and common-control documentation;
- connect requirements to assessment procedures in SP 800-53A;
- select baselines with SP 800-53B;
- create machine-readable control data with NIST OSCAL.

## Critical cautions

- A control catalog is not a complete governance program.
- Controls require selection, tailoring, implementation, assessment, and monitoring.
- NIST states that mappings and crosswalks are not necessarily one-to-one; do not claim equivalence solely from a mapping table.
- The current record should account for Release 5.2.0 issued August 27, 2025.

## Important source locations

- Chapter 2: control concepts, organization-wide risk management, and assurance.

- SP 800-53B: control baselines.
